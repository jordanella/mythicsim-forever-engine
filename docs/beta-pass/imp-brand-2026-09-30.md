# Imp Firebolt and Demonic Brand validation, 2026-09-30

## Status and scope

The engine now follows the build 1.60.1.70124 client contract for Demonic Brand: landed direct pet spells can consume charges, the Imp uses the Fire damage event, other demons use Shadow, the extra hit cannot miss, and charges belong to the branded target and its caster. Regression tests and exact-input replays pass. This is client-data validation, not a claim that Brand has been observed working on the live beta server.

The Imp's level-60 mana pool and regeneration remain unverified beta assumptions. This patch deliberately does not invent a new mana formula, double Firebolt's damage, or infer Improved Imp's hidden dummy effect. The MythicSim production engine pin and boards have not been changed by this validation patch.

## Client evidence

Selected raw rows, source URLs, and full CSV SHA-256 hashes are preserved in `sim/warlock/testdata/imp-brand/client-70124.json`.

- `SpellAuraOptions`, spell 1293696: 100% proc chance, mask 139944 (`0x222a8`). Bits 3, 5, 7, 9, 13 and 17 include taken melee, ranged, and harmful magic hits. A melee-only listener omits the harmful spell category. The caster's pet restriction comes from the tooltip; the server script implementing that filter is not in DB2.
- `Spell`, talent 1293695: Searing Pain brands its target, and the pet consumes charges against that target. The target aura is 1293696. The engine previously kept only a pet buff, so it could spend charges against an unbranded enemy.
- `SpellMisc`: damage spell 1293697 is Shadow (school 32), 1293698 is Fire (school 4). Both carry `Attributes_3 = 262144`, CannotMiss. The old engine instead reported damage as aura 1293696 and rolled a second spell-hit check.
- `SpellDescriptionVariables` 1017 and 1018: level-60 base damage 65 to 68 plus 0.078 times matching school spell power, with pet talent multipliers. Existing owner-SP interpretation and three-times threat are retained assumptions. No new crit behavior is introduced.
- The existing generated talent curves already implement 2/4/6 charges, 10-second duration, and 17/33/50% Searing Pain threat reduction.

Actual consumption uses independent target auras; it cannot leak to another target or another warlock's pet. Damage reports now correctly use 1293697/1293698, so downstream assertions expecting damage under 1293696 must change when adopting this engine.

## Separating the Imp's low damage

1. **Damage per bolt:** baseline normal landed hits average 176.51 damage; crits average 264.65. Its average damage across all landed Firebolts is about 192.91. This includes the reported build's pet talents and target debuffs, not just the raw spell coefficient.
2. **Base Firebolt values:** rank 7 (11763) has 115 mana cost, a 2-second cast, and coefficient 0.571. The build 70124 row has base 44, variance about 0.113636, and 0.6 damage per level above level 58. At level 60 this gives approximately 42.7 to 47.7 before integer rounding, consistent with the existing 43 to 48 model. A tooltip rendered at its max level 63 is not a reason to use its displayed range at level 60. A second dummy effect is not proof of a second damage hit.
3. **Casting frequency:** 29.044 completed casts over an average 119.618-second encounter, about 14.6 per minute. The modeled continuous sequence is one start every 2.2 seconds, including an assumed 200 ms pet delay. The delay is inherited from the earlier simulator and is not independently beta-verified here.
4. **Mana downtime:** the Imp has only 662.4 mana in this build, 576 base times Fel Vitality's 1.15. Its 264 intellect adds no mana because the shared mana setup explicitly leaves pet scaling to the pet, which registers none. In the baseline debug iteration it first falls below the 115 cost at 13 seconds, then resumes at 18 seconds. Across that sampled 130-second fight, gaps between starts exceed the continuous 2.2-second cadence by 54.6 seconds. This is a sample, not an average OOM metric. Judgement of Wisdom and later Life Tap/Demonic Energies refunds materially affect the sequence.
5. **Inherited spell power:** initial owner generic SP is 448, giving the Imp 44.8 inherited SP. Demonic Knowledge separately adds 60 to the pet, producing 104.8 total pet SP. Thus the bolt's raw SP contribution is `104.8 * 0.571 = 59.8408`. The 60 from Demonic Knowledge must not be mistaken for stronger owner inheritance. Dynamic inheritance remains 10% generic owner SP; school-only SP behavior has not been measured in the beta evidence available here.

Classic upstream uses the identical level-60 base stats but gives the Imp a 0.33 power modifier, equivalent to 4.95 mana per intellect above the first 20, plus an Imp-specific spirit-regeneration rule. This demonstrates why simply adding 15 mana per intellect is unsafe. It is a candidate for a separate beta-backed correction, not evidence that Forever retained Classic's rule. Sources: https://github.com/wowsims/classic/blob/master/sim/warlock/imp.go , https://github.com/wowsims/classic/blob/master/sim/warlock/pet.go , https://github.com/wowsims/classic/blob/master/sim/core/mana.go .

## Diagnostic controls, not beta predictions

Both controls used the patched engine and the same 3,000-iteration Kerani input. Go build overlays changed only the indicated constant; neither change is in the committed engine. The overlays must use the canonical `/private/tmp/` source path on macOS.

| Control | Imp DPS | Firebolt casts per fight | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Patched, normal inputs | 65.065 | 29.023 | Reference for both controls |
| Set Imp base mana from 576 to 100000 | 125.373 | 53.965 | Upper bound without mana starvation, not a plausible mana formula |
| Set inherited generic SP fraction from 0.10 to 0 | 54.992 | 29.023 | About 10.073 DPS comes from inherited SP in this input |

The zero-inheritance control keeps Demonic Knowledge's separate 60 pet SP, inherited hit/crit/AP, owner damage, and Brand's owner-SP formula. Its Brand damage is identical to the patched reference; only Firebolt damage falls. The unlimited-mana control increases both Firebolt frequency and opportunities to consume Brand. It must not be used as a ranking result.

## Existing public beta evidence and limits

Inspected Forever Logs' public Warlock rankings and followed actual report links:

- Opsyn, Arugal on September 28: https://foreverlogs.gg/reports/2683/encounters/5808/damage-done?encounters=5808&source=67257 . The selected encounter has owner Corruption, Curse of Agony and Immolate, with no Firebolt sample.
- Nobody, Witherfang: https://foreverlogs.gg/reports/40/encounters/4403/damage-done?encounters=4403&source=33276 . This uses the Succubus Nimnia: 13 melee hits and 2 Lash of Pain hits, not an Imp.
- Report 2681 had no Warlock in its roster.

These low-level reports do not test Demonic Brand, a later Demonology talent. They neither prove nor disprove its server-side behavior and cannot validate a level-60 Imp mana pool. This is a bounded inspection of available public evidence, not a claim that every possible log has been searched.

The firsthand report at https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/spell-ranks-lowering-damage/2356194 describes a low-rank Firebolt tooltip issue. It contains no controlled hit/mana measurements, so it does not justify changing rank-7 damage or doubling the dummy effect.

Remaining measurements, only if further existing exports or logs do not provide them: pet level, intellect, maximum mana and spirit; timestamped Firebolt starts/successes/damage over a sustained fight; Improved Imp rank and owner/pet spell power. A level-20 sample can check low-rank behavior and mana scaling, but cannot certify level-60 damage or a Brand talent unavailable in that build. No new testing request has been posted.

## Validation and reproduction

Regression tests cover Firebolt and Lash consuming charges, preserved melee consumption, no consumption on misses or periodic damage, no owner/foreign-pet consumption, independent targets, refresh and inactive auras, all talent ranks including zero, no recursive Brand hits, no extra miss/crit roll, and matching-school owner SP scaling. Exact replays additionally exercise saved custom APLs and repeated iteration cleanup.

```
go test --tags=with_db ./sim/warlock ./sim/core/spelldata ./cmd/wowsimcli/cmd -count=1
CGO_ENABLED=0 GOOS=linux GOARCH=arm64 go build --tags=with_db -o /tmp/imp-brand-cli ./cmd/wowsimcli
```

For each request, run the compiled CLI in the pinned image using `--entrypoint /audit/wowsimcli`, mount the binary read-only at that path, mount the fixture read-only at `/request.json`, and a writable output directory at `/out`. Use `--network=none --read-only --tmpfs /tmp:rw,size=256m --ulimit cpu=60:60 --memory 1g`, followed by `sim --strict --infile /request.json --outfile /out/result.json`. `replay-results.json` preserves summary metrics and result hashes; full local results and debug logs are in `/tmp/imp-brand-audit/`.
